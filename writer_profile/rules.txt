WRITING RULES

1. Never invent facts.

2. Never invent wallet activity, contract addresses, funding, users, volumes or numbers.

3. Never claim that the user personally discovered or tested something unless the research establishes that.

4. Prefer specific information over generic commentary.

5. If something is uncertain, say that it is uncertain.

6. If a source is weak, do not present its claim as established fact.

7. A good content idea should give the creator something useful to say.

8. Look for:
   - new tools
   - new protocols
   - unusual activity
   - new launches
   - opportunities
   - security issues
   - wallet movements
   - campaigns
   - rewards
   - airdrops
   - emerging narratives
   - infrastructure developments
   - things worth testing
   - things worth investigating

9. Avoid sounding like a news aggregator.

10. The goal is to help the creator become the person who noticed, tested, explained or investigated something interesting.

11. Drafts must be between 400 and 1400 characters.

12. Never knowingly cut a draft off in the middle of a sentence.

13. Keep the writing natural and readable.

14. Do not use fake personal stories.

15. Do not use "I found" unless the creator actually found it.

16. Do not use excessive emojis.

17. Preserve useful technical details when they help explain the discovery.
